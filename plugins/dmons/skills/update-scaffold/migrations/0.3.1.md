# Migration → 0.3.1 — agents never invoke each other

**Applies to:** any repo scaffolded with **0.3.0** (stamped `<!-- dmons-scaffold: 0.3.0 -->`). A repo
coming from 0.2.x applies `0.3.0.md` first, then this note.

## What changed in 0.3.1

Nothing structural — the two nested loops, the roster, and the models are unchanged. This release makes
**one implicit rule explicit**: only the Analyst/Architect (the main thread) invokes agents.

0.3.0 said the Architect "spawns" the reviewer and the supervisor, and told the worker not to hand off
`→ @supervisor`, but it never told the subagents plainly that they must not call each other at all.
Left unsaid, a worker can invoke a reviewer to pre-check its own block, a reviewer can spawn a worker to
apply its findings, and a supervisor can spawn a worker to remediate — each of which quietly runs a loop
the Architect owns, skipping the gates, the box-ticking, and the per-block commit that only the main
thread performs. Every DEVLOG handoff arrow (`→ @reviewer`, `❓ @architect`) is a **post**, not an
invocation.

This note is four small in-place edits and a stamp. All are additive text; none rewrites an existing
section.

## Harvest before applying

Only the `{{UNIT}}` term (see SKILL.md Step 3) — it appears in M3 and M4. Nothing else needs harvesting,
and **nothing needs a fresh audit**.

Also note the **worker roster**: M1 applies to *every* `worker*.md`, one edit per file.

## Migrations, in order

Order doesn't matter here — the edits are independent. Apply M1–M4, then stamp.

### M1 — `worker*.md`: no-invocation boundary *(in-place, additive bullet — every worker file)*

In `## Boundaries — what you must NOT do`, after the "Do not self-approve" bullet:

> - **Never invoke another agent.** You have no authority to spawn `reviewer`, `supervisor`, another
>   `worker`, or any general-purpose subagent — not to check your work, not to parallelise, not to ask a
>   question. **Only the Analyst/Architect (the main thread) invokes agents.** A handoff (`→ @reviewer`)
>   is a DEVLOG post and a line in your report; it is *not* you calling the reviewer. If a block seems to
>   need another agent's help, that is a signal to stop and report to the Architect, not to delegate.

**Idempotency:** if the file already carries a "never invoke" bullet (hand-added), leave it and report
the migration as already current.

### M2 — `reviewer.md`: add a `## Boundaries` section *(in-place, additive section)*

0.3.0's `reviewer.md` has no `## Boundaries` heading — its constraints are scattered inline ("You
report; you do not edit"). Append a section at the **end of the file**, after `## Do not approve when`:

> ## Boundaries
>
> - **You report; you do not edit.** Never fix what you find — the worker applies the fixes and you
>   re-audit.
> - **Do not tick or untick `tasks.md` boxes**, and do not commit, amend, or revert anything.
> - **Never invoke another agent.** You have no authority to spawn a `worker`, the `supervisor`, or any
>   general-purpose subagent — not to fix a finding, not to get a second opinion, not to escalate.
>   **Only the Analyst/Architect (the main thread) invokes agents.** `❓ @architect` and `→ @worker` are
>   DEVLOG posts, not agent calls. If a finding needs someone else to act, post it and report it; the
>   Architect routes the work.

The first two bullets restate rules already present inline in 0.3.0 — that repetition is deliberate, so
the boundary list is complete in one place. **Do not delete the inline statements** to compensate.

If the repo has already grown its own `## Boundaries` in `reviewer.md`, add only the third bullet to it.

### M3 — `supervisor.md`: no-invocation boundary *(in-place, additive bullet)*

In the existing `## Boundaries`, after the "Do not tick or untick" bullet:

> - **Never invoke another agent.** You have no authority to spawn a `worker`, the `reviewer`, or any
>   general-purpose subagent — not to remediate a finding, not to re-review a block, not to parallelise
>   reading the {{UNIT}}. **Only the Analyst/Architect (the main thread) invokes agents.** Your output is
>   a DEVLOG post and a report; the Architect carves the remediation block and calls whoever implements
>   it.

### M4 — `CLAUDE.md`: the Architect is the sole invoker *(in-place, additive paragraph)*

In `## Roles`, immediately **before** the closing "All agents are defined for this repo. Delegate; don't
shortcut…" line:

> **You are the only agent that invokes agents.** The `worker`s, the `reviewer`, and the `supervisor`
> never spawn each other or any other subagent — they report back to you and you route the next step.
> Every handoff in the DEVLOG (`→ @reviewer`, `❓ @architect`) is a *post*, not an invocation: the
> reviewer runs when **you** spawn it, the supervisor runs when **you** spawn it at {{UNIT}} end. This
> keeps one thread holding the whole picture — if an agent could call the next one, the workflow's loops
> would run without you and the gates, ticks, and commits you own would be skipped.

Substitute the repo's `{{UNIT}}` term. If the repo has a single full-stack worker, "The `worker`s" reads
"The `worker`".

## In-flight changes

Harmless mid-change — the rule constrains who calls whom, not what any agent produces, so a change
part-built under 0.3.0 needs no note beyond the usual SKILL.md Step 7 one-liner. Nothing in an existing
DEVLOG becomes wrong.
